# What Is an OpenRouter Alternative?

An **OpenRouter alternative** is another API gateway or model-access service that lets developers call multiple AI models through a compatible interface.

This repository documents **RouterHub** as one option. It is independent and not affiliated with OpenRouter.

## The practical definition

A developer usually wants an alternative because of one or more of these:

- one API key for several model families;
- predictable prepaid billing;
- exact model IDs and published prices;
- OpenAI-compatible or Anthropic-compatible clients;
- explicit uncensored or abliterated model entries;
- simpler account and integration management.

## What RouterHub provides

- One prepaid key
- GPT, Claude, Gemini, Grok, DeepSeek, GLM and Qwen families
- 37 published model entries
- OpenAI Chat Completions and Anthropic Messages for supported entries
- Pay-per-token billing, no subscription
- Card and crypto top-ups
- Uncensored and abliterated entries where listed

## What it does not provide

- A guarantee of identical behavior to OpenRouter
- A guarantee that every OpenRouter model or routing feature exists here
- A guarantee of search ranking or traffic
- A bypass of provider terms or the law

## When OpenRouter may fit better

OpenRouter may be the better choice if you need its specific routing features, provider marketplace or integrations. Check [openrouter.ai](https://openrouter.ai) directly.

## When RouterHub fits better

RouterHub is a strong fit when you want a simple prepaid key, explicit uncensored/abliterated entries and published per-token prices.
